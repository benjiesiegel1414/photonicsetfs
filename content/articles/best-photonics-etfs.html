How to choose between them

Start with what you actually want to own. If you want the companies building optical links for AI data centers, look at the funds focused on optical interconnects and transceivers. If you want the light-based tools that make chips, the lithography-focused fund is a different and broader bet.

Then look at how each fund is run. An index fund like {{link:PHOX}} follows fixed rules. Actively managed funds can change holdings quickly, and some can own private companies or use swaps. Neither is better by default, but you should know which one you are buying.

Finally, compare costs and size. Fees range from {{exp:@cheapest}} to {{exp:@priciest}} a year. Bigger funds tend to trade with tighter spreads. You can line up any two funds in our head-to-head comparisons.

Whatever you pick, remember these are all new, concentrated, single-theme funds. Size positions accordingly and read each prospectus.
