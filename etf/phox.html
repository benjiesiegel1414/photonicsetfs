What PHOX is

The Aura AI Photonics ETF tracks the VettaFi AI Photonics Index, a basket of roughly 25 global companies involved in generating, transmitting and processing data with light for AI computing: lasers, transceivers, photonic integrated circuits and specialty materials such as indium phosphide.

Worth knowing

As an index fund, PHOX follows set rules rather than a manager's calls, which makes it easier to know what you own. It is still a small, non-diversified, single-theme fund, so expect big swings.
