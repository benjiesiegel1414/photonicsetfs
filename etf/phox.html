PHOX at a glance

The Aura AI Photonics ETF tracks the VettaFi AI Photonics Index, a basket of roughly 25 global companies involved in generating, transmitting and processing data with light for AI computing: lasers, transceivers, photonic integrated circuits and specialty materials such as indium phosphide.

  • Size: about $2M in assets, #7 of 9 photonics ETFs.
  • Cost: 0.60% a year, cheaper than 6 of the other 8 funds. The group average is 0.69%.
  • How it is run: Index (passive), tracking the VettaFi AI Photonics Index.
  • Concentration: the three largest positions add up to about 23% of the fund (Coherent, Zhongji Innolight, Credo Technology).
  • Track record: 4 months of trading history, so there is no long-term record to judge yet.
China-listed exposureHolds or references stocks listed in mainland China.

Worth knowing

As an index fund, PHOX follows set rules rather than a manager's calls, which makes it easier to know what you own. It is still a small, non-diversified, single-theme fund, so expect big swings.

How PHOX invests

PHOX is the only pure photonics ETF in the U.S. that tracks a published index. The VettaFi AI Photonics Index weights companies by how central photonics is to their business and by market value, then rebalances on a schedule.

The index reaches beyond the laser and module makers into the networking chips and connectors that sit next to them, which is why Broadcom, Arista, Credo and Amphenol show up in the top ten alongside Coherent, Lumentum and the Chinese transceiver makers.

Because the rules are public, you can see ahead of time what the fund will own and when it will change. The trade-off is that nobody is steering around a problem stock between rebalances.

Read the prospectus and the latest holdings on the Aura ETFs website.

What PHOX holds

The top three positions make up about 23% of PHOX. Its published top holdings overlap most with LYTE (5 shared names), LUMA (4 shared names), LAZR (3 shared names). See every stock the photonics ETFs own and how much they overlap.

PHOX fees

PHOX charges 0.60% a year. On a $10,000 position that is about $60 a year, $300 over five years and $600 over ten, before any gains or losses. The same $10,000 in the cheapest photonics ETF (EUV at 0.35%) would cost $350 over ten years, and the most expensive (LUMA at 1.00%) would cost $1,000.

PHOX size

With about $2M in assets, PHOX ranks #7 of 9 photonics ETFs. That is small. Small funds can trade with wider bid-ask spreads, and issuers sometimes close funds that do not gather assets, so it is worth checking the spread and using limit orders.

Who PHOX may fit

It may fit if

  • You want fixed, published rules instead of a manager's judgment
  • You want a flatter spread across the top ten than the active funds offer
  • You want optical networking names such as Broadcom, Arista and Credo in the mix

It may not fit if

  • You need a fund with heavy daily trading volume; PHOX is very small
  • You want only pure photonics companies; several holdings are broader networking names
  • You want to avoid China-listed stocks

General education only, not a recommendation. Your own goals, timeline and risk tolerance decide what fits.

PHOX risks to understand

  • Very small funds can trade at wide spreads and face closure risk if they do not grow
  • Index rules can force buying after big run-ups
  • China-listed holdings add policy and currency risk
  • Like every fund on this site, PHOX is a new, single-theme ETF. Photonics stocks fell hard in October 2026, with several of the biggest names dropping 10% or more in a day. Read our guide to photonics ETF risks before sizing a position.

PHOX FAQ

What is PHOX?

PHOX is the Aura AI Photonics ETF, a photonics-focused ETF from Aura ETFs. The Aura AI Photonics ETF tracks the VettaFi AI Photonics Index, a basket of roughly 25 global companies involved in generating, transmitting and processing data with light for AI computing: lasers, transceivers, photonic integrated circuits and specialty materials such as indium phosphide.

What is PHOX's expense ratio?

PHOX charges 0.60% a year, about $60 for every $10,000 invested. That ranks #3 of 9 photonics ETFs, cheapest first.

How big is PHOX?

PHOX has about $2M in assets under management, #7 of 9 photonics ETFs by size.

When did PHOX launch?

PHOX launched on August 10, 2026 and trades on NYSE Arca.

What does PHOX hold?

As of Nov 2026, the largest positions were Coherent, Zhongji Innolight, Credo Technology, Eoptolink, Lumentum. Percent of net assets. Holdings change, so check the issuer's website for the current list.

Is PHOX actively managed or an index fund?

PHOX is an index fund. It tracks the VettaFi AI Photonics Index.

Does PHOX pay a dividend?

Distribution schedule: Not declared yet. Photonics companies mostly reinvest their cash, so growth-focused photonics ETFs pay little or nothing. Check the issuer's distribution history for actual payments.

Which photonics ETF is most similar to PHOX?

Based on published top holdings, PHOX shares the most names with LYTE (Coherent, Zhongji Innolight, Eoptolink, Lumentum). You can compare them side by side on our PHOX vs LYTE page.

How do I buy PHOX?

PHOX trades like a stock on NYSE Arca. You can buy it in most brokerage accounts by searching the ticker PHOX. Newer, smaller ETFs can have wide bid-ask spreads, so many investors use limit orders.

Is PHOX a good investment?
