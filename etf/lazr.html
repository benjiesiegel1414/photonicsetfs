LAZR at a glance

The Tema Photonics & Optical ETF invests in global photonics and optical technology companies tied to AI data movement, including optical interconnect, semiconductor technology and related equipment and materials. Tema launched it with research input from semiconductor analysis firm SemiAnalysis.

  • Size: about $276M in assets, #1 of 9 photonics ETFs, or roughly 40% of all the money in the funds we track.
  • Cost: 0.75% a year, cheaper than 2 of the other 8 funds. The group average is 0.69%.
  • How it is run: Actively managed.
  • Concentration: the three largest positions add up to about 42% of the fund (Lumentum, Aixtron, Anthropic (private, via SPV)).
  • Track record: 5 months of trading history, so there is no long-term record to judge yet.
Holds private companiesSome positions are private companies valued by estimate, not a daily market price.

Worth knowing

LAZR has also held a position in privately held AI company Anthropic, which is unusual for an ETF. Private positions are valued differently from listed stocks and can be harder to trade, so read the prospectus on how they are handled.

How LAZR invests

LAZR is a global, actively managed fund with about 25 holdings. It goes further up the supply chain than most of its peers: alongside Lumentum and Coherent it owns the companies that make the raw materials and tools for optical chips, such as Aixtron (deposition equipment), AXT (indium phosphide wafers), LandMark Optoelectronics and Win Semiconductors.

It is also the fund with the biggest single position in the group. Lumentum was more than a fifth of the portfolio in the issuer's November 2026 holdings file.

The fund can put a slice of its assets in private companies. Its largest private position is exposure to Anthropic held through a special purpose vehicle, which the issuer marks using secondary-market pricing.

Read the prospectus and the latest holdings on the Tema website.

What LAZR holds

The top three positions make up about 42% of LAZR. Its published top holdings overlap most with LUMA (5 shared names), PHOX (3 shared names), EUV (2 shared names). See every stock the photonics ETFs own and how much they overlap.

LAZR fees

LAZR charges 0.75% a year. On a $10,000 position that is about $75 a year, $375 over five years and $750 over ten, before any gains or losses. The same $10,000 in the cheapest photonics ETF (EUV at 0.35%) would cost $350 over ten years, and the most expensive (LUMA at 1.00%) would cost $1,000.

LAZR size

With about $276M in assets, LAZR ranks #1 of 9 photonics ETFs. That puts it among the largest funds in this young category, which usually means tighter spreads and more daily volume.

Who LAZR may fit

It may fit if

  • You want the materials and equipment layer of photonics, not only the module makers
  • You like a high-conviction manager willing to hold one large top position
  • You want a little private-company exposure inside an ETF wrapper

It may not fit if

  • You want an even spread across holdings
  • You do not want private positions that cannot be priced daily on an exchange
  • You want a fund that sticks only to U.S.-listed stocks

General education only, not a recommendation. Your own goals, timeline and risk tolerance decide what fits.

LAZR risks to understand

  • One stock above 20% of the fund means single-company news moves the whole ETF
  • Private holdings are marked by estimate and can be hard to sell
  • Foreign small caps in Taiwan, Germany and China add currency and liquidity risk
  • Like every fund on this site, LAZR is a new, single-theme ETF. Photonics stocks fell hard in October 2026, with several of the biggest names dropping 10% or more in a day. Read our guide to photonics ETF risks before sizing a position.

LAZR FAQ

What is LAZR?

LAZR is the Tema Photonics & Optical ETF, a photonics-focused ETF from Tema. The Tema Photonics & Optical ETF invests in global photonics and optical technology companies tied to AI data movement, including optical interconnect, semiconductor technology and related equipment and materials. Tema launched it with research input from semiconductor analysis firm SemiAnalysis.

What is LAZR's expense ratio?

LAZR charges 0.75% a year, about $75 for every $10,000 invested. That ranks #7 of 9 photonics ETFs, cheapest first.

How big is LAZR?

LAZR has about $276M in assets under management, #1 of 9 photonics ETFs by size.

When did LAZR launch?

LAZR launched on June 29, 2026 and trades on NYSE.

What does LAZR hold?

As of Nov 20, 2026, the largest positions were Lumentum, Aixtron, Anthropic (private, via SPV), AXT Inc, LandMark Optoelectronics. Percent of net assets. Holdings change, so check the issuer's website for the current list.

Is LAZR actively managed or an index fund?

LAZR is actively managed, so a portfolio manager chooses the holdings and their sizes instead of following an index.

Does LAZR pay a dividend?

Distribution schedule: Not stated by the issuer. Photonics companies mostly reinvest their cash, so growth-focused photonics ETFs pay little or nothing. Check the issuer's distribution history for actual payments.

Which photonics ETF is most similar to LAZR?

Based on published top holdings, LAZR shares the most names with LUMA (Lumentum, Aixtron, LandMark Optoelectronics, Coherent). You can compare them side by side on our LAZR vs LUMA page.

How do I buy LAZR?

LAZR trades like a stock on NYSE. You can buy it in most brokerage accounts by searching the ticker LAZR. Newer, smaller ETFs can have wide bid-ask spreads, so many investors use limit orders.

Is LAZR a good investment?

We do not make buy or sell recommendations. Compare its strategy, fees and size with the other photonics ETFs and read the prospectus before deciding.
