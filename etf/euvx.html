EUVX at a glance

The Corgi Lithography & Semiconductor Photonics 2x Daily ETF aims for twice the daily move of its lithography and semiconductor photonics strategy.

  • Size: about $2M in assets, #8 of 9 photonics ETFs.
  • Cost: 0.45% a year, cheaper than 7 of the other 8 funds. The group average is 0.69%.
  • How it is run: 2x daily leveraged.
  • Track record: 6 months of trading history, so there is no long-term record to judge yet.

Worth knowing

Leveraged ETFs reset every day. Over periods longer than a day, returns can differ a lot from 2x the underlying move, especially in choppy markets. These are trading tools, not long-term holdings for most investors.

How EUVX invests

EUVX does not own a basket of stocks. It holds a total return swap that references the unleveraged EUV fund, sized at roughly 200% of assets, with cash and Treasury holdings behind it.

The goal is twice the return of EUV for a single day. The fund rebalances its swap exposure at the end of every trading day to get back to 2x.

That daily reset is why the long-run result is not simply double. If EUV falls 10% one day and rises 10% the next, EUV is down 1% while a 2x fund is down 4%. The more the underlying fund whipsaws, the wider that gap gets.

Why 2x daily is not 2x over time

What happensUnleveraged fund2x daily fund
Down 10% one day, up 10% the next-1.0%-4.0%
Ten days alternating -5% and +5%-1.2%-4.9%
Up 5% two days in a row+10.3%+21.0%

These are simple math examples, not forecasts. They show why a leveraged fund can lose ground in a choppy market even when the fund it follows ends up close to flat, and why it can do better than double in a steady trend.

EUVX fees

EUVX charges 0.45% a year. On a $10,000 position that is about $45 a year, $225 over five years and $450 over ten, before any gains or losses. The same $10,000 in the cheapest photonics ETF (EUV at 0.35%) would cost $350 over ten years, and the most expensive (LUMA at 1.00%) would cost $1,000.

EUVX size

With about $2M in assets, EUVX ranks #8 of 9 photonics ETFs. That is small. Small funds can trade with wider bid-ask spreads, and issuers sometimes close funds that do not gather assets, so it is worth checking the spread and using limit orders.

Who EUVX may fit

It may fit if

  • You are an active trader with a view on the next day or the next few days
  • You understand daily-reset leverage and watch positions closely

It may not fit if

  • You plan to buy and hold for months or years
  • You would be uncomfortable with a 20% or larger loss in a single week
  • You want a fund you do not need to monitor

General education only, not a recommendation. Your own goals, timeline and risk tolerance decide what fits.

EUVX risks to understand

  • A large one-day drop in EUV is doubled
  • Volatility decay eats returns in choppy markets even if EUV ends flat
  • Tiny asset base and swap counterparty risk
  • Like every fund on this site, EUVX is a new, single-theme ETF. Photonics stocks fell hard in October 2026, with several of the biggest names dropping 10% or more in a day. Read our guide to photonics ETF risks before sizing a position.

EUVX FAQ

What is EUVX?

EUVX is the Corgi Lithography & Semiconductor Photonics 2x Daily ETF, a photonics-focused ETF from Corgi Funds. The Corgi Lithography & Semiconductor Photonics 2x Daily ETF aims for twice the daily move of its lithography and semiconductor photonics strategy.

What is EUVX's expense ratio?

EUVX charges 0.45% a year, about $45 for every $10,000 invested. That ranks #2 of 9 photonics ETFs, cheapest first.

How big is EUVX?

EUVX has about $2M in assets under management, #8 of 9 photonics ETFs by size.

When did EUVX launch?

EUVX launched on June 3, 2026 and trades on Cboe BZX.

Is EUVX actively managed or an index fund?

EUVX is a leveraged fund that targets a multiple of the daily move of its underlying strategy. It does not track a traditional index.

Does EUVX pay a dividend?

Distribution schedule: Annual. Photonics companies mostly reinvest their cash, so growth-focused photonics ETFs pay little or nothing. Check the issuer's distribution history for actual payments.

Why does EUVX use swaps?

Swaps let a fund get the return of a stock or index without holding the shares directly, which can be useful for foreign stocks or leverage. The trade-off is counterparty risk: the fund depends on the bank on the other side of the swap.

How do I buy EUVX?

EUVX trades like a stock on Cboe BZX. You can buy it in most brokerage accounts by searching the ticker EUVX. Newer, smaller ETFs can have wide bid-ask spreads, so many investors use limit orders.

Is EUVX a good investment?

We do not make buy or sell recommendations. Compare its strategy, fees and size with the other photonics ETFs and read the prospectus before deciding.
